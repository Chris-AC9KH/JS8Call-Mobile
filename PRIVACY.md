# Privacy Policy for JS8Call-Mobile

**Effective Date:** November 11, 2026  
**Developer:** Chris Olson (AC9KH)  
**Repository / Homepage:** [https://github.com/Chris-AC9KH/JS8Call-Mobile/](https://github.com/Chris-AC9KH/JS8Call-Mobile/)

## Overview

JS8Call-Mobile is an amateur radio utility for iPadOS. Your privacy is important, and this app is designed to operate completely under your local control without collecting or transmitting personal data to the developer or third parties.

## Data Collection and Tracking

JS8Call-Mobile **does not collect, store, transmit, or share** any personal information, analytics, crash telemetry, location tracking data, or usage metrics with the developer or any third-party services.

## Local Storage on Your Device

All station configuration settings you enter into the app—including your amateur radio callsign, Maidenhead grid square, TCI server IP address and port, audio offset preferences, and custom auto-reply messages—are stored strictly locally on your iPad using Apple's standard `UserDefaults` storage. This information never leaves your device except when transmitted over the air as standard amateur radio protocol frames by your own transceiver.

## Microphone and Audio Access

When local audio mode is enabled, JS8Call-Mobile requests access to your iPad's microphone (`AVAudioApplication`) solely to process live audio signals in real time for decoding and encoding JS8 digital mode transmissions. Audio is processed exclusively in memory on your device and is never recorded, saved to disk, or uploaded to external servers.

## Local Network Access

JS8Call-Mobile uses local network permissions solely to establish a direct connection to the user-configured TCI (Transceiver Control Interface) server IP address and port on your local network to exchange radio frequency, waterfall spectrum, and digital audio data.

## Third-Party Services

JS8Call-Mobile does not integrate any third-party advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or external cloud APIs.
